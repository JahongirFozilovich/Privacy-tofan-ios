--- meta-viewport: width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 title: Tofan Workout - Privacy Policy --- # Privacy Policy Effective Date: 18.09.2026 **Tofan Workout** (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates the Tofan Workout mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information. ## 1. Information We Collect ### Account Information - Username - Password (securely hashed) - Email address - Phone number ### Profile Information - First name (optional) - Last name (optional) - Age - Gender - Weight - Height ### Media Data - Images selected or captured by the user for AI meal analysis (optional) ### Health Data (Apple Health) With your explicit permission, we access the following data types through Apple HealthKit: - Steps and distance - Active and resting energy (calories) - Heart rate - Workout sessions and duration - Body weight and height Health data is used solely to power in-app fitness tracking and progress features. It is never used for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes, in accordance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines. ## 2. How We Use Your Data - Provide workout and fitness features - Sync and display fitness activity from Apple Health - Analyze meal images for nutrition estimation - Maintain user accounts - Contact you regarding account, security, or service updates (email / phone number) - Improve app performance ## 3. Data Storage Your data is stored securely using Firebase services provided by Google. Access is restricted using Firebase security rules. Health data obtained via Apple HealthKit is processed on-device and in our secure backend only as needed to provide app features. ## 4. Permissions Used - Activity Recognition - Camera - Storage / Media Access - Foreground Service - Boot Completed - Internet Access - Notifications - Vibration - Apple HealthKit (read/write, with user permission) ## 5. Data Deletion Users can request account deletion directly inside the app at any time. - All user profile data is permanently removed from Firebase - All fitness and activity data is deleted - All related media data is removed - Health data synced from Apple Health is deleted from our systems (data stored in the Apple Health app itself is managed separately by the user through iOS Settings) Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. ## 6. Data Sharing We do not sell or rent personal data, including health data. Data is only processed within Firebase infrastructure or when required by law. ## 7. Children's Privacy This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. ## 8. Your Rights - Access your data - Request deletion - Revoke Apple Health permissions at any time via iOS Settings - Stop using the app anytime ## 9.
